The Story

Started in a
garage. Still
in a garage.

XPRST started because I kept seeing people wear the same generic brands while carrying wildly specific ideas around in their heads. You had a phrase you loved. A joke only your friend would get. A memory you wanted on your chest instead of buried in a notes app.

So I bought a press. Now I take whatever's on your mind and put it on a garment that outlasts the thought that inspired it.

No minimums. No templates. No "choose from our 40 designs" nonsense. You bring the idea. I bring the press, the ink, and the patience to get it right.

Right now I work out of a small space. It's just me, a press, a cut-out table, and a stack of blanks. It's not a factory. That's the point.

The workspace. Small but every piece earns its place.

How It Works

Three steps.

01

Send It

DM me your idea. A phrase, a photo, a hand-drawn sketch, a song lyric — whatever it is. I'll ask clarifying questions and send back a mockup.

02

Approve

You get a mockup on the exact garment, in the exact color, in the exact size. If something's off, I redo it. You don't pay until you say "that's it."

03

Wear It

I press and inspect. Pick up locally or it goes in the mail. First wash: cold, inside out, no dryer. It'll outlast your attention span.
